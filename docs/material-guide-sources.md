# Material explanation references
General material-family context; not ARCOS test results or product certifications.
- GFRC thin-section and complex geometry: https://www.grca.org.uk/pdf/congress-2015/21%20Advancing%20the%20architectural%20application%20of%20complex%20geometry%20GFRC.pdf
- UHPC strength and durability: https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/innovation/everydaycounts/edc_6/uhpc_bridge_preservation.cfm

Products formerly labelled Concrete now show GFRC / UHPC, with the formulation to be confirmed for the design, rather than implying conventional concrete or assigning an unverified specific mixture.
Fold process story uses the verified V12 R1 two-stage model. Sketch, prototype and mould images are generated illustrations of the workflow, not photographs documenting completed fabrication. Silicone and vacuum-formed plastic are alternative tooling routes, selected by geometry.
